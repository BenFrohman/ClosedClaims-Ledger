# Row 6 is not filled

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman. Copyright (c) 2026. Apache-2.0.

The declaration

    instance (D) (h : D.codim = 2) : CycleSection D where
      construct := ?z_of
      is_section := ?cl_z_eq

is the shape of Term A. It is not a term of Term A until both fields exist for every such D and every Hodge class: surfaces Z_i and rationals a_i with cl(sum a_i [Z_i]) = gamma.

named_fourfolds fills a finite list. That is not every D.
gamma.val / rfl is the dummy.
sorry is a hole.
cl = 0 on Rat is a gadget.

No construction of those surfaces for variable D exists in this program or in the literature as a finished theorem. Filling Row 6 by grind would be proving the Hodge conjecture. That proof is not written here.
